## 📌 Purpose

This index defines how Bluewater architecture decision records are proposed, accepted, superseded, and retained.

## Record lifecycle

Decision records use four-digit sequence numbers and Markdown files. A proposed record documents context, considered options, the recommended decision, consequences, security implications, and validation. Accepted records are immutable except for factual corrections and status changes.

A later decision may supersede an accepted record. The old record remains available and links to its replacement so the historical reasoning is preserved. Rejected proposals may also remain when their context prevents repeated investigation.

## Current records

No implementation decision has yet been imported into this cleaned publication tree. New records must start from the decision template and describe behavior implemented or deliberately planned through an approved change.
